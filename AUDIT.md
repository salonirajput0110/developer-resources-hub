GDG AITR Portal Audit

Overview

I reviewed the GDG AITR portal on desktop and mobile to check its
layout, navigation, usability and overall user experience.

Testing

I checked the main navigation, links, buttons, scrolling, mobile
responsiveness, contact information, footer and overall visual
consistency.

I did not find any major functional issues during normal testing.

Observations and Suggestions

1. Event section

The event section could show more of the actual work and activities
done by the platform instead of mainly presenting information through
numbers and text.

Suggestion:
Add a small image slider/carousel in the Events section showing
photos and highlights from previous events. This would make the
section more visual and engaging.

2. College address

The college address in the footer/contact section is not directly
connected to a map.

Suggestion:
Make the address clickable and link it to the college location on
Google Maps so visitors can easily find the venue and get directions.

3. Email address

The email shown in the footer/contact section could be easier to use.

Suggestion:
Make the email address clickable using a mail link so that clicking
it opens the user's email application.

4. Social media

The footer or contact section could provide easier access to the
official social media accounts.

Suggestion:
Add Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), GitHub and other available
social media icons in the footer. Each icon should link to the
corresponding official account.

5. FAQ / Help section

There is no dedicated section for common questions from new visitors.

Suggestion:
Add an FAQ or Help section, preferably in the main navigation or
near the bottom of the homepage. It can answer questions about
events, registration, membership, participation and other common
queries.

6. Contact the team

There could be a clearer way for visitors to ask questions or contact
the GDG AITR team.

Suggestion:
Add a "Contact the Team" section in the footer or main navigation
with the official email, relevant contact details and, if suitable,
a simple contact form.

7. Light and dark mode

The portal currently does not have a light/dark mode option.

Suggestion:
Add a light/dark mode toggle in the top navigation or another
clearly visible place. This would give users more control over the
appearance of the portal.

Positive observations

- The portal has a clean and modern interface.
- The main navigation is easy to understand.
- The website works well on desktop and mobile during normal testing.
- The content is presented in a simple and organized way.

Conclusion

The portal worked well during my testing and I did not find any major
functional issues. The suggestions above mainly focus on improving
visual content, communication, accessibility and the overall user
experience.
